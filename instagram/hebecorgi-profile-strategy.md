# @hebecorgi — Profile Rebuild ("Follow in 5 Seconds")

> **Assumption:** @hebecorgi is a pet-creator account for a corgi named Hebe.
> The live profile could not be fetched (Instagram blocks automated access), so
> Section 1 is an audit checklist to score the current profile against, and
> Sections 2–6 are the ready-to-paste rebuild. Swap any detail that doesn't fit.

---

## The 5-second test

A stranger landing on the profile must answer three questions instantly:

| Second | Question | Answered by |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 | **Who is this?** | Profile photo + name field |
| 1–3 | **What do I get if I follow?** | Bio line 1 (the promise) |
| 3–5 | **Is it good?** | Pinned trio + first grid row |

If any one of those is unclear, they leave.

## 1. Audit — score the current profile

Mark each ✅ / ❌. Every ❌ is fixed by the section noted.

**Name field** (→ §2)
- [ ] Contains a searchable keyword (e.g. "Corgi", "Dog"), not only "Hebe" or an emoji
- [ ] Is 40 characters or fewer, so it doesn't cut off on mobile
- [ ] Does not repeat the handle word for word (wastes the search slot)

**Bio** (→ §3)
- [ ] Line 1 is a promise to the viewer ("you get X"), not a description of the dog
- [ ] States what kind of content and how often it posts
- [ ] Has personality (voice) and one clear call to action
- [ ] No hashtag lists, no "DM for collabs" as the lead line

**Pinned posts** (→ §4)
- [ ] 3 posts are pinned
- [ ] One introduces Hebe, one proves reach (best performer), one shows the recurring format
- [ ] Covers have readable text in the safe zone (center 1:1 of a 4:5/9:16 frame)

**Highlights** (→ §5)
- [ ] 5 or fewer, all with matching covers
- [ ] Titles are 10 characters or fewer, so they aren't cut off
- [ ] First highlight is "who is Hebe", not an old trip or event

**Link** (→ §6)
- [ ] One goal per link, with the top link matching the bio's call to action
- [ ] Link page loads fast on mobile and has 3 buttons or fewer

**Profile photo**
- [ ] Hebe's face fills 70%+ of the circle, bright background, readable at thumbnail size

## 2. Name field (searchable, 64-character limit)

Instagram search indexes the **name field** and the handle, not the bio. Use it for the keyword.

**Recommended:**
```
Hebe the Corgi 🐾 Funny Dog Videos
```

Alternatives by niche:
- Comedy: `Hebe | Corgi Comedy & Dog Memes`
- Lifestyle / city: `Hebe the Corgi | [City] Dog Life`
- Training / tips: `Hebe the Corgi | Corgi Tips & Tricks`

**Primary keyword:** `corgi`. **Secondary:** `funny dog`, `dog videos`, `[city] dog`.
Use the same keywords in reel captions and on-screen text. Instagram's search reads those too.

---

## 3. Bio (150-character limit)

Formula: **promise → proof or personality → posting rhythm → call to action**

**Recommended (entertainment):**
```
Your daily dose of corgi chaos 🍞
Short legs. Big opinions.
New reels Tue • Thu • Sat
👇 Hebe's fave gear + merch
```

**Alternative (community / relatable owner angle):**
```
If you own a corgi, you'll feel seen 😅
Real corgi life, zero filter
3 reels a week 🐾
👇 Start here
```

**Alternative (collabs are the goal):**
```
Corgi comedy that makes your day better 🐾
Seen on [feature/press]
New reels 3x a week
📩 Collabs: link below
```

Rules:
- Line 1 is about the **viewer**, not the dog.
- Make one promise only. Don't list five things.
- The posting rhythm tells people following is worth it. Only include it if you'll keep it.
- Put emails and "DM for collabs" in the contact button or link page, not the bio.

## 4. Pinned post trio (left → right)

| Slot | Role | What it is | Cover text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Meet Hebe** (who) | 15–30s reel: name, age, 3 quirks, "follow for…" at the end | "Meet Hebe 🐾" |
| 2 | **Best hit** (proof) | Your highest-reach reel ever. Leave it as is | Hook from the reel ("She did NOT agree to this") |
| 3 | **Signature series** (what you'll keep getting) | Latest episode of the recurring format (see below) | "[Series name] Ep. X" |

Signature series ideas (pick one and post it weekly):
- **"Hebe Rates…"**: Hebe reacts to foods or toys, with a score out of 10
- **"Corgi vs."**: Hebe against stairs, the vacuum, snow, a cat
- **"Loaf of the Week"**: a follower-submitted corgi loaf photo (builds community)

Keep slots 2 and 3 current. Swap slot 2 whenever a reel beats it, and slot 3 every new episode.

## 5. Highlight covers plan

Keep **5 highlights max**, in this order (most important first):

| # | Title | Contents | Icon |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Meet Hebe` | Intro, birthday, fun facts, FAQ ("Is she fluffy?") | Paw |
| 2 | `Series` | Best clips from the signature series | Star |
| 3 | `Daily` | Walks, zoomies, behind the scenes | Sun |
| 4 | `Gear` | Harness, toys, food she uses, with links or codes | Bone |
| 5 | `Collabs` | Past brand work, results, how to work together | Handshake |

Cover design system:
- One solid **brand color** background (pick one: warm corgi orange `#F4A259` or cream `#FFF3E0`)
- One **simple line icon** in white or dark brown, centered, same stroke weight
- No photos on covers (too busy at small size) and no text on the cover itself
- Size 1080×1920, icon inside the center 400×400 area
- Make all 5 in one Canva template, so new covers always match

Delete or archive any highlight about a one-time event older than 6 months.

## 6. Link strategy

**Goal order:** 1) grow the audience, 2) earn money through affiliates or merch, 3) land brand deals.

**Option A: native Instagram links (up to 5).** Recommended if you have 3 goals or fewer.
1. `Hebe's fave gear` → affiliate storefront (Amazon Storefront or LTK)
2. `Merch` → shop (only if it exists)
3. `Work with Hebe` → one-page media kit (Canva or Notion)

The **first link** is the only one visible on the profile. Make it match the bio's 👇 call to action.

**Option B: link-in-bio page** (Linktree, Beacons, Stan). Use it if you need tracking or email capture.
- Keep it to 3 buttons, with the same order and labels as above
- Top: Hebe's photo + one sentence that repeats the bio promise
- Add a free **"Corgi Starter Guide"** PDF for email sign-ups. This builds an audience you own
- Check click counts monthly and delete any button with less than 5% of clicks

Don't:
- Link to another social account at the top. It sends people away from the one you're building
- Use 8+ buttons. Too many choices means no click

---

## 7. First-row grid rule

The top 3 grid posts under the pins are the second thing people judge. Make sure:
- Hebe's face is in every cover frame
- Hooks are readable at thumbnail size
- The first 9 posts include no reposts, memes without Hebe, or low-light photos

## 8. Rollout checklist (one afternoon)

1. [ ] Update the profile photo (tight crop, bright background)
2. [ ] Change the name field (§2)
3. [ ] Paste the new bio (§3)
4. [ ] Record and post the "Meet Hebe" reel, then pin slots 1–3 (§4)
5. [ ] Build the 5 highlight covers, reorder and prune highlights (§5)
6. [ ] Set up links / the link page (§6)
7. [ ] Screenshot before and after. Track profile visits → follows (Insights) for 30 days

**Success metric:** profile-visit → follow conversion. Typical pet accounts sit at 5–10%. Aim for **15%+**.
